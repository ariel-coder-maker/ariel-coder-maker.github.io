OUTBREAK LAB ESCAPE

How it works. Six locked rooms. Each room has 4 question objects. Each correct answer gives your team a letter. Unscramble the 4 letters into a word and enter it at the door to win that room’s key. Each room has 6 minutes. After that, every full minute late turns one of your six team members into a zombie, and they stay that way.

Think before you click. A wrong answer adds 15 seconds to your clock. Clear a room with no wrong answers and you find an antidote 🧪: it cures one infected team member, or stops the next infection.

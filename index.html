What I'm building

My first MYP year came with no materials and four classes to plan. So I stopped prompting a chatbot lesson by lesson and built a system instead. The school goes in once: its structure, calendar, criteria, standards and lab stock. After that, a short request gets a unit, a lesson package or a lab order that already fits the school.

Curriculum generator

Units planned against IB criteria and AERO standards, checked against what's actually in the lab, with every version kept.

Lesson packages

Slides, worksheets, a teacher manual and lab setup guide, shaped to each teacher's preferences.

Lab order list

Every practical across five grades cross-checked with stock, so the order form writes itself.

Student feedback

Anonymised submissions in, grading sheets and personalised feedback worksheets out.

What to do on Monday

  1. Photograph your filled-in template and upload it to your AI tool.
  2. Ask it to turn box A into a folder structure for your school.
  3. Add one thing at a time: calendar, then criteria, then standards.
  4. Ask for one lesson for one class you teach this week. When it misses, fix the stored school info, not the prompt.
